# What to test — beta focus areas

This is the doc that lives behind the link, where I as the developer
can think clearly about what I want to learn from each tester.

It's organized as a single 3–4 week stress test plan, not "test
everything every day." Each week has a focus.

---

## Week 1 — First impressions and setup

What I want to know:

1. Does the **setup flow** feel intuitive? Did you finish it without
   confusion? Did anything feel weird to enter? Did the
   personalisation questions feel invasive or fine?
2. Does the **home screen** make sense in the first few seconds? Is
   the streak counter the first thing your eye lands on? Does the
   "Today" headline feel meaningful?
3. Did the **disclaimer screen** feel appropriate or annoying? Does
   it give you confidence the app isn't pretending to be a doctor?
4. Did the **BETA pill** show up where you expect it? Did you notice
   it within a minute of using the app?
5. **Privacy posture** — does the app *feel* private? Anything that
   makes you suspicious it isn't?

How to send feedback for week 1: tap the BETA pill once you've
finished setup. Type three lines about anything that surprised you.

## Week 2 — The craving moment

The whole point of this app is being good in the moment a craving
hits. I want to know if the "I'm craving" flow actually works.

1. Tap the **floating "I'm craving" button** at least once. Does it
   feel obvious? Does it feel like a panic button, in a good way?
2. Run a **15-minute timer** to completion. Does the breathing pulse
   help? Are the tips useful or noise? Do you naturally want to look
   at the timer every minute, or does it disappear from your mind?
3. Try a **calming game** mid-timer. Which one did you pick? Did it
   actually distract you?
4. Use the **slip flow** at least once — even if you didn't actually
   slip. Walk through pretending. Does the "I had a setback" copy
   feel kind, or does it feel like the app is lecturing you? Does
   the 3-step recovery flow feel useful?

## Week 3 — The journal and patterns

The data side. Does logging feel worthwhile?

1. **Log at least 5 journal entries** during the week. After 5+ entries
   you should see the predictive insights cards. Are they accurate?
   Surprising? Useful? Noise?
2. Try the **mood week** dots. Does it feel like a meaningful weekly
   summary or visual fluff?
3. Use the **daily check-in** when it pops up. Does the timing feel
   natural? Do the emotion options cover what you'd want to log?
4. Read the **daily prompt** above the journal log. Does it land for
   you? Are the prompts varied enough or do they feel repetitive
   already?

## Week 4 — Long-term feel and edge cases

By now you've used the app for ~3 weeks. What I want to know:

1. **Has any data been lost?** Have you noticed an entry disappearing
   between sessions? A streak resetting unexpectedly? This is the
   single most important question — the persistence layer is new.
2. **Theme experimentation.** Have you tried more than one? Do any
   feel particularly right? Particularly wrong?
3. **Settings deep dive.** Have you explored every setting? Anything
   that feels broken or missing? Did you successfully Export a
   backup, then Import it? (Try it.)
4. **What would you actually pay $4.99/month for?** When the trial
   ends, would you reach for your wallet? If not, what's missing?
5. **What would you want first** in a v2?

## How to send feedback

There are two paths and both go to me directly:

1. **BETA pill (top-right corner)** → tap it → "Send feedback." Opens
   your email app with a pre-filled subject and the build version
   embedded. Type your feedback above the divider line.
2. **Settings → Privacy & policies → "Send beta feedback"** — same
   thing, slightly different starting point.

You can send as many as you want. Even one-line feedback like
*"this button felt weird"* is more useful than no feedback.

---

## Things I genuinely cannot see

So you can be honest without worrying:

- I do not see your journal entries
- I do not see what addictions you're tracking
- I do not see your slip records
- I do not see your emergency contact
- I do not see how often you open the app
- I do not see crashes (no analytics installed yet)

The only thing I receive is the email you choose to send me, with
whatever you choose to write in it. Nothing more.
