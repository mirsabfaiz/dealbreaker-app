to send to friends and family. Replace `[BETA_URL]` with your actual
Vercel deployment URL.

## What to send if someone asks "what is this for?"

Some friends won't have a personal recovery context but still want to
help. Reply with:

> Honestly anyone can test it — the value is in whether the
> experience *feels* right, not whether you're tracking real
> behavior. Pick a category that feels relevant to you (or just pick
> "social media" or "porn" if nothing else fits). Use it for a week.
> Tell me what's confusing, what's missing, what feels good. That's
> the whole ask.

---

## What to do if they report a bug

Most feedback will come via the BETA pill → Send feedback. The email
arrives with the build hash pre-filled, so you can correlate to the
exact deploy.

For more involved bug reports, ask:

1. What did you tap right before it broke?
2. iPhone or Android? Which model if you remember?
3. Could you take a screenshot of the screen where it happened?
4. Is the build hash from the BETA pill what you see in the
   feedback email? (If not, they're on an older cached version —
   tell them to fully close the app and reopen.)
