## Long description

Deal Breaker is a private recovery tracker for people who don't want
their struggle to live on a public feed.

There's no community wall. No daily public pledge. No social pressure.
Just a calm, honest space for the work.

**Multi-addiction by design.** Track alcohol, cannabis, nicotine,
hard drugs, gambling, porn, and more — all in one place, each with
its own streak. Or just track the one thing you're working on.

**Built for the moments that matter most.**
- Tap *I'm craving* and a 15-minute timer starts, with distraction
  tips and a guided breathing pulse to ride the wave.
- Three calming mini-games for when you need to focus your hands.
- A 3-step soft path for when you slip — no shame, just a way back.

**Patterns, gently surfaced.** As you log cravings and check-ins,
Deal Breaker quietly notices things — your peak craving time, your
highest-risk day, the emotion that shows up before slips, and how
this week compares to last. Insights, not lectures.

**Your data stays on your device.** No account. No login. No analytics
SDK. No cloud syncing of your recovery information. The only thing
that leaves your phone is the standard subscription receipt that
Apple or Google handles. We never see your journal entries.

**Themes for your mood.** Six dark and light palettes — from quiet
twilight purple to soft warm paper. Pick what feels right today.

**Quiet by design.**
- No social feed
- No leaderboards
- No public pledges
- No identity required
- No sharing buttons that lead anywhere but where you want them to

**Pricing.** 14-day free trial, then $4.99/month. Cancel anytime.

---

**Deal Breaker is not a medical device or treatment platform. If you
are in crisis, please contact a qualified professional or crisis
service. In the US, dial 988.**

> {feature/fix} to make the app a little more useful while you do.

## Age rating recommendations

Both stores will run you through a content questionnaire. The honest
answers for Deal Breaker are:

- Substance use references: yes (the app is *about* recovery from
  substance use; users describe their own struggles)
- No explicit imagery, no gambling mechanics, no purchasing other
  than the subscription itself

Result: typically **17+** on App Store, **Mature 17+** on Play Store.
This is fine — your audience is adults.
