# How to delete your Deal Breaker data

Deal Breaker stores all of your data on your device. We do not store
your recovery information on any server. To delete your data:

1. Open the Deal Breaker app
2. Tap **Settings** (bottom navigation)
3. Scroll to the bottom and tap **Reset everything**
4. Confirm

Your data is wiped from device storage immediately. Because we never
had a copy of your data on our servers, there is nothing further to
delete.

## If you also want to stop your subscription

Subscription billing is handled by Apple App Store or Google Play —
not by us. Cancel through your store account:

- **iPhone / iPad:** Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Deal
  Breaker → Cancel Subscription
- **Android:** Google Play app → Profile icon → Payments &
  subscriptions → Subscriptions → Deal Breaker → Cancel

Cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing period.

## If you want to delete your data without keeping the app

Uninstall the app. On both iOS and Android, uninstalling removes the
app's local storage entirely.
