# Health Disclaimer

Deal Breaker is a personal tool for tracking habits and milestones in
your own recovery journey. **It is not a medical device, therapy
program, or treatment platform.** It is not staffed by clinicians and
is not designed to diagnose, treat, or cure any condition.

The streaks, prompts, patterns, and insights shown in the app are
generated from your own logged data. They are reflections, not
medical advice.

If you are struggling with addiction, mental health, or thoughts of
self-harm, please reach out to a qualified professional. Real human
support — a therapist, doctor, sponsor, friend, or hotline — is more
helpful than any app.

## What this app is, and isn't

**Is:**
- A private way to track time clean
- A way to surface patterns from your own logged data
- A 15-minute craving timer with distraction tips
- A space to journal anonymously
- A reminder of milestones along the way

**Isn't:**
- A substitute for a counselor, doctor, or sponsor
- A diagnostic tool
- A crisis service
- A guarantee of any particular outcome
