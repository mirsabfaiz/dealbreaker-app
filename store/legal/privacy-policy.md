# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** [EFFECTIVE_DATE]
**Last updated:** [LAST_UPDATED]

## Plain-English summary

Deal Breaker is built so that the data you put into it stays on your
device. We do not collect, see, transmit, or store your recovery
information on our servers. There is no account. There is no login.
There is no analytics tracking inside the app.

The only information that leaves your device is the standard
subscription receipt that Apple or Google generates when you sign up
for the trial or paid tier. We use that receipt only to verify your
subscription status — never to identify you personally.

If you can read just one section, this is the one.

## 2. What information we process

### 2.1 Stored on your device only (we never see it)

When you use Deal Breaker, the following information is stored locally
in your device's app storage. **It does not leave your device unless
you explicitly export it yourself.**

- The categories of substance use or behavior you choose to track
- The dates and times associated with each category
- Journal entries you write, including any free-text notes
- Slip records and the emotions / situations you log alongside them
- Personal milestones you set
- Optional emergency contact name and phone number you save
- Daily-cost figures you enter
- Optional answers to the personalisation questions
- Your chosen theme and notification preferences
- Backup files you generate via the in-app Export feature (saved
  to wherever you choose, by you)

We have no access to any of this. There is no API call from the app
to our servers carrying this data. There is no analytics SDK
recording what you write.

### 2.2 Subscription receipts (handled by Apple / Google)

When you start your free trial or pay for the subscription:

- Apple App Store or Google Play handles the entire transaction.
- Apple/Google issue a receipt confirming you have a valid trial or
  paid subscription.
- We use that receipt — and only that receipt — to unlock the
  subscription features in the app.
- We do not receive your name, email, payment details, or any other
  identifying information from Apple or Google.

If we use a third-party receipt validation service (RevenueCat or
similar), that service receives the same minimal receipt and does not
receive any of your in-app recovery data.

### 2.3 Crash and diagnostic information

The app does not currently include any crash reporting SDK. If a
future version adds one (for stability monitoring), this policy will
be updated and the SDK will be configured so it does not capture the
contents of any text fields, journal entries, or other recovery data.

### 2.4 Information we never collect

For absolute clarity, Deal Breaker does **not**:

- Collect your name, email, phone number, or any contact information
- Collect or transmit any precise location data
- Use cookies, web beacons, or any other tracking technology
- Sell or share data with advertisers
- Use any "behavioral analytics" SDK that records taps, scrolls, or
  text input
- Maintain user accounts or profiles on any server
- Cross-reference your usage with any other app or service

## 3. How information is used

The information stored on your device is used only to power the app's
features for you — counting your streak, surfacing patterns from your
journal, celebrating your milestones, and similar. None of it is used
for any other purpose.

The subscription receipt is used only to determine whether you have an
active subscription and to display the corresponding state in the app.

## 4. Sharing

Because we do not collect your data, we have nothing to share. We will
never sell, rent, or trade your data because we don't have it to sell.

We share subscription receipts only with the App Store / Play Store
that issued them, and (if used) with our receipt-validation
service for the sole purpose of confirming subscription status.

## 5. Your data, your control

Even though your data is on your device, the GDPR, CCPA, and similar
laws give you specific rights. We honor those rights via the app's
built-in features:

- **Right to access:** Use Settings → Export Backup to download a
  complete copy of all your data as a JSON file.
- **Right to portability:** The exported JSON file is yours. Take it
  to another app, store it, or share it as you see fit.
- **Right to deletion:** Use Settings → Reset Everything to delete all
  your data instantly. The data is wiped from device storage. We
  never had a copy on a server, so there is nothing else to delete.
- **Right to rectification:** All entries can be edited or removed in
  the app at any time.
- **Right to object / restrict processing:** Stop using the app, or
  uninstall it. Both immediately stop all processing on your device.

You do not need to email us to exercise any of these rights — the
app provides them directly. If you'd prefer to contact us anyway,
we'll respond at [SUPPORT_EMAIL] within 30 days.

## 6. Children's privacy

Deal Breaker is not directed at children under 13 (under 16 in the
EU). We do not knowingly collect any data from children. If you
believe a minor has provided personal information, contact us at
[SUPPORT_EMAIL] and we will respond appropriately — though again, we
hold essentially no data, so the practical action is for the child or
their guardian to use Settings → Reset Everything on their device.

## 7. Security

Your data is stored in your device's app storage, which is protected
by your device's operating system. On modern iOS and Android devices,
this storage is encrypted at rest by default when your device has a
passcode. We strongly recommend setting a device passcode and
enabling biometric unlock.

We deliberately do not back up your data to Google Drive or iCloud
because doing so would route highly sensitive information through
third parties. If you want to migrate to a new device, use Settings →
Export Backup, then Settings → Import Backup on the new device.

## 8. International users
